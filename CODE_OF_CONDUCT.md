# 社区行为准则

## 我们的承诺

我们致力于为所有参与者提供友善、尊重、包容且专业的协作环境，不因经验、身份、背景、语言或观点而区别对待。

## 期望行为

- 尊重不同观点和经验；
- 提供具体、建设性且与项目相关的反馈；
- 对自己的言行及其影响负责；
- 在分歧中聚焦事实、技术权衡和用户需求；
- 保护他人的隐私、作品和机密信息。

## 不可接受的行为

- 骚扰、威胁、侮辱、歧视或带有敌意的言论；
- 人身攻击、持续挑衅或故意破坏讨论；
- 未经许可公开他人的私人信息；
- 发布机密演示文稿、个人数据、访问凭据或漏洞利用细节；
- 任何在专业协作环境中不合理的行为。

## 执行

维护者可以编辑或删除不当内容，限制参与，临时暂停或永久禁止严重或重复违规者。处理措施将考虑行为的严重程度、影响和历史，并尽可能保持一致和适度。

如需报告行为问题，请通过 [@LilRayyyy 的 GitHub 主页](https://github.com/LilRayyyy) 联系维护者。不要在公开 Issue 中发布敏感信息。维护者会在合理范围内保护报告者隐私。

本准则适用于仓库、Issue、Pull Request、评审、讨论以及代表本项目参与的其他公共空间。

# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, inclusive, and professional environment for every participant, regardless of experience, identity, background, language, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Respect different perspectives and experiences.
- Give specific, constructive, project-relevant feedback.
- Take responsibility for your words, actions, and their impact.
- Keep disagreements focused on evidence, technical trade-offs, and user needs.
- Protect other people's privacy, work, and confidential information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, discrimination, or hostile language;
- personal attacks, sustained provocation, or deliberate disruption;
- publishing private information without permission;
- publishing confidential decks, personal data, credentials, or vulnerability details;
- conduct that would be unreasonable in a professional collaboration environment.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, restrict participation, temporarily suspend, or permanently ban serious or repeated violations. Responses will consider severity, impact, and history and will be proportionate whenever possible.

Report conduct concerns through [@LilRayyyy's GitHub profile](https://github.com/LilRayyyy). Do not post sensitive details in a public Issue. Maintainers will protect reporter privacy within reasonable limits.

This policy applies to the repository, Issues, Pull Requests, reviews, discussions, and other public spaces where someone represents the project.
